Corporate desk

Company advertising, without a campaign attached.

This desk is for any company that might work with Twenty Block Media — brands, local and regional businesses, and agencies — on advertising, media, or partnerships that are not political.

If the work is a candidate, a PAC, or a Dem-aligned cause, that belongs on the Political desk. If the work is Matthew’s first-person story, that belongs on Person.

What this desk is for

  • Corporate advertising and media sales
  • Partnership conversations with businesses and agencies
  • Non-political placement and related media work

What will not appear in a corporate deck

  • Democratic campaign creative
  • Candidate endorsements
  • Matthew’s medical or ERISA narrative as a brand case study

Wall. Political invoices and Person pitches stay off this desk. A company should not have to read around a campaign, and a campaign should not be billed as a brand.
