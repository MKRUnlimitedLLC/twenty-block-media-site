Person desk

Matthew Rau’s story, kept on its own desk.

This desk is first-person work. It exists for editors, producers, and literary and national desks — not for rate cards, sponsorship decks, or campaign asks.

Twenty Minutes is a feature in draft: illness, a long-term disability fight, and the years as a baseball general manager. Related media lives here too. The through-line is a usable twenty-minute window of focus, and a life that had to be rebuilt inside it.
